## System Architecture

### Hong Kong: Dual-Track, Public-Dominant
```
Government (Food & Health Bureau)
    │
    ├── Hospital Authority (HA) — 43 hospitals, 74% beds, 90% inpatient days
    │   ├── 7 clusters (geographic)
    │   ├── Heavily subsidized (HK$180/day inpatient)
    │   └── Training ground for specialists
    │
    ├── Department of Health — Primary care, prevention, port health
    │   ├── 73 general outpatient clinics (GOPC)
    │   ├── Maternal & child health centres
    │   └── Elderly health centres
    │
    └── Private Sector — 12 hospitals, 26% beds, 60% specialists
        ├── High-end, insurance-driven
        ├── Medical tourism focus
        └── VHIS (Voluntary Health Insurance Scheme) regulation
```

### Singapore: 3M + Integrated Shield, State-Steered
```
Ministry of Health (MOH)
    │
    ├── Public Clusters (3) — NUHS, NHG, SingHealth
    │   ├── 11 acute hospitals, 80%+ subsidized for citizens
    │   ├── Polyclinics (23) — primary care anchor
    │   └── Community hospitals, specialty centres
    │
    ├── 3M Framework (Financing)
    │   ├── MediSave (mandatory savings, 8-10.5% wages)
    │   ├── MediShield Life (basic catastrophic, auto-enrolled)
    │   └── MediFund (safety net, means-tested)
    │
    ├── Private Sector — 9 hospitals, 20% beds
    │   ├── Integrated Shield Plans (IP) — private coverage
    │   ├── Medical tourism (300k+ patients/yr)
    │   └── Raffles, Mount Elizabeth, Parkway, Gleneagles
    │
    └── Regulatory — MOH licenses, MediSave accreditation, fee benchmarks
```

## Medical Tourism

### Hong Kong
- **Arrivals**: ~50k/yr (pre-COVID), growing post-border reopening
- **Source**: Mainland China (80%), SE Asia, expats
- **Strengths**: Cardiac, oncology, orthopedics, reproductive
- **Price**: 30-50% below US, comparable to SG
- **Visa**: Medical visa 90 days, extendable

### Singapore
- **Arrivals**: ~300k-500k/yr (pre-COVID), rebounding
- **Source**: Indonesia (40%), Myanmar, Vietnam, China, Middle East
- **Strengths**: Oncology, cardiology, neurology, transplant, fertility
- **Price**: 20-40% below US, premium to regional peers
- **Brand**: "Medical Hub of Asia", JCI accreditation standard

## Digital Health & Innovation

### Hong Kong
- **eHRSS**: Electronic Health Record Sharing System (public + private opt-in)
- **HA Go**: App for appointments, records, prescriptions (2M+ users)
- **Telemedicine**: Accelerated post-COVID, private-led
- **AI/Research**: HKU/CUHK medical AI, CUHK Medical Centre (smart hospital)
- **Greater Bay Area**: Cross-border data pilot (Shenzhen-HK)

### Singapore
- **NEHR**: National Electronic Health Record (mandatory contribution)
- **HealthHub**: National portal/app (appointments, records, MediSave, screenings)
- **Telemedicine**: Licensed providers, MOH sandbox, chronic care focus
- **AI/Research**: AI in screening (SELENA+), precision medicine (PRECISE), NUHS/NHG innovation
- **Smart Nation**: Sensor-based eldercare, Health District @ Queenstown

## Key Challenges

### Hong Kong
1. **Public system overload**: Aging + population growth + staff shortage
2. **Specialist brain drain**: Private sector pays 3-5x, emigration wave 2020-2023
3. **Primary care weakness**: GOPC overcrowded, no gatekeeping, fragmentation
4. **Mental health**: Long waits, stigma, limited community support
5. **Cross-border integration**: GBA health cooperation nascent, regulatory gaps

### Singapore
1. **Cost inflation**: Medical inflation 8-10%/yr, MediShield premiums rising
2. **Aging tsunami**: 1 in 4 >65 by 2030, long-term care capacity gap
3. **Workforce burnout**: Nurse attrition, doctor hours, locum dependence
4. **MediSave adequacy**: Withdrawal limits vs rising private costs
5. **Inequality in access**: PRs/foreigners pay more, private gap widening

## Future Directions

### Hong Kong: "Primary Care Reform" + "GBA Integration"
- **District Health Centres (DHCs)**: 18 districts, gatekeeping, chronic care
- **VHIS expansion**: Mandatory? Employer-mandated?
- **Private hospital regulation**: Price transparency, package pricing
- **GBA medical zone**: Hetao Shenzhen-HK innovation, cross-border insurance
- **AI/triage**: HA smart scheduling, radiology AI, predictive analytics

### Singapore: "Healthier SG" + "Age Well SG"
- **Healthier SG**: Enroll with 1 GP, preventive focus, fully subsidized screenings
- **Age Well SG**: Active aging centres, home care, community networks
- **MediShield Life review**: Higher limits, premium sustainability
- **Long-term care**: CareShield Life (severe disability), eldercare integration
- **Precision medicine**: Genomics, data analytics, preventive stratification

## Bottom Line

| Choose Hong Kong If | Choose Singapore If |
|---------------------|---------------------|

| Medical tourism from Mainland China | Medical tourism from SE Asia/Middle East |
