## System Architecture

### Hong Kong: MTR-Centric, Property-Rail Integration
```
MTR Corporation (Listed, Govt majority owner)
    │
    ├── Heavy Rail (10 lines): Kwun Tong, Tsuen Wan, Island, Tung Chung,
    │   Airport Express, Disneyland, Tseung Kwan O, East Rail, South Island,
    │   Tuen Ma (West Rail + Ma On Shan merged)
    │
    ├── Light Rail (LRT): 68 stops, NW New Territories (Tuen Mun/Yuen Long)
    │
    ├── Airport Express: 24 min HK-Central, in-town check-in
    │
    ├── Cross-Border: Lok Ma Chau, Lo Wu, Heung Yuen Wai (to Shenzhen)
    │   └── High-speed rail (XRL): West Kowloon → Guangzhou (48 min), Shenzhen (14 min)
    │
    ├── Property Development: "Rail + Property" model
    │   ├── Podium developments at stations
    │   ├── Profits fund rail expansion (no govt capex)
    │   └── 40%+ revenue from property/rental
    │
    └── Subsidiaries: MTR (Shenzhen), MTR (UK), MTR (Australia), MTR (Sweden)
```

### Singapore: LTA-Planned, Multi-Operator, Bus-Rail Integration
```
Land Transport Authority (LTA) — Statutory board, plans/regulates/funds
    │
    ├── Rail Operators (concession contracts):
    │   ├── SMRT Trains (NSL, EWL, CCL, TEL, BPL) — Temasek-owned
    │   └── SBS Transit (NEL, Sengkang/Punggol LRT, DTL) — ComfortDelGro
    │
    ├── Bus Operators (bus contracting model since 2016):
    │   ├── SBS Transit, SMRT Buses, Tower Transit, Go-Ahead
    │   ├── LTA owns assets (buses, depots), pays operators fees
    │   └── Service standards enforced, data shared
    │
    ├── Active Mobility: 500km park connectors, cycling paths, paths
    │
    ├── Demand Management: ERP 2.0 (satellite, distance-based), COE, parking
    │
    └── Future: Cross-border RTS Link (Woodlands-JB, 2026), HSR (deferred)
```

## Fare Systems

### Hong Kong: Octopus (Stored Value, Ubiquitous)
- **Coverage**: MTR, all buses, minibuses, trams, ferries, trams, parking, retail (7-Eleven, supermarkets, vending)
- **Penetration**: 35M+ cards (4.5 per capita), 16M+ daily transactions
- **Fare structure**: Distance-based (rail), flat/zoned (bus), transfer discounts (MTR-bus)
- **Concessions**: Elderly $2 (all modes), students 50%, disabilities
- **Technology**: FeliCa (Sony), NFC, Apple Pay/Google Pay/Samsung Pay integration
- **Auto-top-up**: Credit card linked, threshold-based

### Singapore: SimplyGo / EZ-Link / NETS FlashPay
- **SimplyGo**: Account-based, credit/debit card direct (Mastercard/Visa), no card needed
- **EZ-Link**: Stored value card, transit + retail + motoring (ERP, parking)
- **NETS FlashPay**: Stored value, NETS network, motoring
- **Concessions**: Concession cards (student, NSF, senior, PwD) - physical card required
- **Fare structure**: Distance-based (integrated rail+bus), transfer rules (45 min, 5 transfers)
- **Technology**: CEPAS 3.0 (account-based), SimplyGo mobile app

## Active Mobility

### Hong Kong
- **Cycling**: Limited urban cycling (narrow roads, no lanes), recreational in New Territories (cycle tracks 60km+), folding bikes on MTR (off-peak)
- **Walking**: Extensive covered walkways, footbridges, subways (Central-Mid-Levels escalator 800m)
- **Policy**: "Walking first" rhetoric, but car-centric road design persists
- **Shared mobility**: Limited dockless (geo-fenced), e-scooters banned on roads/footpaths

### Singapore
- **Cycling**: 500km park connectors, 130km cycling paths (target 1,300km by 2030), cycling towns (Tampines, Ang Mo Kio), bikes on MRT (folding always, full-size off-peak)
- **Walking**: Covered linkways (200km+), sheltered paths to MRT, "Friendly Streets" pilot
- **Policy**: "Walk Cycle Ride" masterplan, car-lite vision, Active Mobility Act (e-scooters regulated)
- **Shared mobility**: Licensed operators (Anywheel, HelloRide, Neuron), geo-fenced parking, strict enforcement

## Air Connectivity

### Hong Kong International Airport (HKG)
- **Pre-COVID**: 71M pax, 120 airlines, 220 destinations
- **2024 recovery**: ~70% of 2019, rebuilding long-haul
- **Cargo**: #1 globally (4.5M tonnes), 50% by value
- **Connections**: 45 min to Central (Airport Express), SkyPier ferry-to-air
- **Expansion**: 3-runway system (2024), 100M capacity target
- **GBA integration**: Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macau Bridge (HZMB) bus/shuttle

### Singapore Changi Airport (SIN)
- **Pre-COVID**: 68M pax, 100 airlines, 380 destinations
- **2024 recovery**: ~90% of 2019, strong rebound
- **Cargo**: #7 globally (1.8M tonnes), high-value pharma/electronics
- **Connections**: 30 min to city (MRT), Jewel lifestyle hub
- **Expansion**: Terminal 5 (2030s, 50M pax), 3-runway system
- **Hub strategy**: 5th freedom rights, SQ network, low-cost terminal (T4)

## Innovation & Future

### Hong Kong
- **Smart stations**: Facial recognition entry, AI crowd management
