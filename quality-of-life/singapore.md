# Quality of Life: Singapore Deep Dive

## Overview
Singapore is a master-planned city-state where urban design, governance efficiency, and multicultural management create a distinctly orderly, green, and livable environment — often called "Asia for beginners" but with deep sophistication beneath the surface.

## Housing & Living Space
- **Homeownership**: 89% (HDB flats), among world's highest
- **HDB flats**: 1M+ units, 99-year leases, ethnic integration quotas
- **Median HDB resale**: ~S$550k (4-room), ~S$750k (5-room)
- **Private condos**: ~S$1.8M median, 22% of households
- **Land scarcity**: 734 km², aggressive land reclamation (+25% since 1965)
- **Average wait for BTO**: 3-5 years (varies by estate)

## Daily Life Experience
**Transportation**
- MRT: 200+ km, 6 lines, expanding to 360km by 2030
- Bus network: 300+ routes, 5,800 stops, integrated fares
- SimplyGo/ EZ-Link: Contactless, account-based ticketing
- Car ownership: COE system (S$80-100k for Cat A), 10-year quota
- Cycling: 500km park connectors, expanding cycling paths

**Food & Dining**
- Hawker centres: 110+ centres, 6,000+ stalls, UNESCO heritage
- Michelin: 50+ stars, including hawker stalls (Hong Kong Soya Sauce Chicken)
- Food delivery: GrabFood, Foodpanda, Deliveroo ubiquitous
- Dining out: Affordable (S$3-6 hawker), high frequency

**Leisure & Green Space**
- "City in Nature" vision: 300+ parks, 4 nature reserves
- Park connectors: 360km linking parks island-wide
- Gardens by the Bay, Botanic Gardens (UNESCO), Mandai wildlife precinct
- Beach access: East Coast, Sentosa, Pulau Ubin
- Mandatory greenery: LUSH/Greenery Replacement policies

## Healthcare System
- **3M Framework**: MediSave (mandatory savings), MediShield Life (basic insurance), MediFund (safety net)
- **Public**: 11 acute hospitals, 80%+ subsidized for citizens
- **Private**: 9 hospitals, premium service, medical tourism hub
- **Outcomes**: Life expectancy 83.9, healthy life expectancy 73.6
- **Costs**: Citizen inpatient ~S$30-80/day (B2/C wards), private ~S$500-1,500/day
- **MediSave**: 8-10.5% of wages (age-banded), usable for family

## Education
- **Local system**: 6+4+2/3, PSLE → O/A Levels or IB, streaming being phased out
- **Bilingual policy**: English + Mother Tongue (Mandarin/Malay/Tamil)
- **International schools**: 60+, IB/UK/US/Aus, S$30-50k/year
- **Universities**: NUS, NTU top 20 globally; SMU, SUTD, SUSS specialized
- **SkillsFuture**: Lifelong learning credits (S$500 base + top-ups)

## Work Culture
- **Standard hours**: 9am-6pm, 44h/week statutory max
- **Tripartite Guidelines**: Flexible work, right to disconnect (2024)
- **4-day week**: Pilot programs, growing adoption
- **Annual leave**: 7-14 days statutory, 14-28 typical professional
- **CPF**: 20% employer + 20% employee (age-banded), 37% total <55
- **Foreign workforce**: EP/S Pass/Work Permit, quotas + levies

## Social Dynamics
- **Population**: 5.92M (3.6M citizens, 0.5M PR, 1.8M non-residents)
- **Ethnic mix**: Chinese 74%, Malay 13%, Indian 9%, Other 3% (citizen pop)
- **Gini coefficient**: 0.433 (after taxes/transfers: 0.371)
- **Aging**: 19% 65+, fertility 1.05, heavy immigration reliance
- **Governance**: PAP dominance since 1959, clean (CPI #5 globally)
- **Social compact**: Meritocracy + multiculturalism + self-reliance + community

## Strengths
1. Clean air, green city, planned spaciousness
2. Housing security via HDB (89% ownership)
3. Governance efficiency, policy continuity, low corruption
4. Multicultural harmony (managed, institutionalized)
5. Work-life initiatives (flexible work, 4-day week trials)
6. Medical tourism hub, high-quality healthcare
7. English-first, easy expat integration
8. Safe, low crime, high trust

## Weaknesses
1. Tropical heat/humidity year-round (no seasons)
2. High car costs (COE), limited land
3. HDB lease decay (99-year, no freehold)
4. CPF lock-up (retirement age 65, withdrawal limits)
5. Conformity pressure, "kiasu" culture
6. Limited political opposition space
7. Dependence on foreign labor (tensions)
8. Cost creep: GST 9%, carbon tax rising
