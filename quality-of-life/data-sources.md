## Methodology Notes

### Cost Comparisons
- **Currency**: HKD/USD pegged (7.75-7.85), SGD free-floating (~1.33-1.36)
- **PPP**: IMF PPP conversion factors used for real comparisons
- **Basket**: Professional single expat, mid-range lifestyle
- **Housing**: 1BR rental in "expat-preferred" districts (Mid-Levels/Central vs River Valley/Holland Village)

### Index Limitations
- **Mercer/EIU**: Expat-focused, weight housing heavily, may not reflect local experience
- **Numbeo**: Crowdsourced, selection bias, but good for relative costs
- **HDI**: National aggregates, mask intra-city inequality
- **Happiness**: Survey-based, cultural response bias possible

### Data Currency
- **Government stats**: Typically 1-2 year lag for annual reports
- **Real-time**: Transport, air quality, property transactions
- **Indices**: Annual (mostly 2023-2024 releases cited)
- **This repo**: Data compiled Q4 2024, verify before decisions

## Methodology for This Repo

1. **Triangulation**: Cross-reference government + international + private sources
2. **Recency weighting**: Prioritize 2023-2024 data, note lags
3. **Contextualization**: Explain structural vs cyclical factors
4. **Transparency**: Flag where data is contested or methodological differences exist
5. **Actionability**: Frame for decision-making, not just observation

## Known Data Gaps

- **HK subdivided housing**: Official count ~110k units, NGOs estimate 200k+
- **SG migrant worker wellbeing**: 1.4M work permit holders, limited QoL data
- **Mental health**: Stigma affects reporting in both, HK improving survey coverage
- **Longitudinal expat tracking**: Most surveys cross-sectional
- **Post-COVID normalization**: 2022-2024 data volatile for commuting, office, retail
