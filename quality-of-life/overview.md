## Key Differentiators

### Hong Kong Strengths
- **Density & Energy**: Unmatched urban vitality, 24/7 city
- **Nature Access**: 40% country parks, hiking from city center
- **Cultural Depth**: Unique East-West fusion, Cantonese heritage
- **Healthcare Outcomes**: World-leading life expectancy (85.5 years)
- **Public Transit**: MTR — 99.9% on-time, profitable, comprehensive

### Singapore Strengths
- **Urban Planning**: Master-planned, green, spacious by design
- **Governance**: Clean, efficient, long-term policy horizon
- **Air Quality**: Consistently better (PM2.5 ~12 vs ~20 μg/m³)
- **Housing Security**: HDB system = 89% homeownership
- **Multilingual Harmony**: Four official languages, managed diversity
- **Work-Life Initiatives**: 4-day work week trials, right to disconnect

## Summary Verdict

**Singapore wins on** measurable liveability indices: air quality, housing security, governance efficiency, work-life initiatives, multicultural integration.

**Hong Kong wins on** raw urban energy, nature proximity, healthcare outcomes, cultural distinctiveness, transit excellence.

**Choose Hong Kong if**: You prioritize urban intensity, outdoor access, Cantonese culture, low taxes, and can navigate density/housing costs.

**Choose Singapore if**: You value clean air, planned spaciousness, institutional stability, family-friendly systems, and multicultural ease.
