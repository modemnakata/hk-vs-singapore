# Quality of Life: Hong Kong Deep Dive

## Overview
Hong Kong delivers an intense, high-octane urban experience with surprising nature access. It's a vertical city where 7.5M people occupy just 25% of the land area — the rest is country parks, reservoirs, and mountains.

## Housing & Living Space
- **Median apartment size**: ~430 sq ft (private), ~250 sq ft (subdivided)
- **Homeownership rate**: ~50% (vs 89% Singapore)
- **Public housing**: ~30% of population (PRH + subsidized sale)
- **Cage homes/subdivided flats**: ~110,000 units, ~220,000 residents
- **Average wait for PRH**: 5.6 years (general applicants)

## Daily Life Experience
**Transportation**
- MTR: 99.9% punctuality, 10 lines, 99 stations, 270km
- Fare recovery ratio: ~180% (profitable)
- Octopus card: 99% penetration, used for retail/parking
- Minibuses, trams, ferries, peak trams complement MTR

**Food & Dining**
- 15,000+ restaurants, 70+ Michelin stars
- Dai pai dong (street food) culture fading but iconic
- Wet markets in every district
- High dining-out frequency: avg 13 meals/week outside home

**Leisure & Nature**
- 24 country parks (443 km² = 40% land area)
- 4 long-distance hiking trails (MacLehose, Wilson, Lantau, Hong Kong Trail)
- 41 beaches, 8 geopark sites
- Hiking from Central to Victoria Peak in 30 min

## Healthcare System
- **Public**: Hospital Authority, 43 hospitals, heavily subsidized (HK$180/day inpatient)
- **Private**: 12 hospitals, world-class, expensive (HK$5,000-15,000/day)
- **Insurance**: VHIS (Voluntary Health Insurance Scheme) tax-deductible
- **Outcomes**: Life expectancy 85.5 (F: 88.1, M: 83.0) — world #1
- **Medical tourism**: Growing, especially from mainland

## Education
- **Local system**: 6+3+3+4, HKDSE exam, high pressure
- **International schools**: 50+, IB/UK/US curricula, HK$150-300k/year
- **Universities**: HKU, CUHK, HKUST top 50 globally
- **Medium of instruction**: Cantonese (local), English (EMI schools + intl)

## Work Culture
- **Standard hours**: 9am-6pm/7pm, often later in finance/legal
- **Overtime culture**: Prevalent, often unpaid
- **Annual leave**: 7 days (statutory) → 14+ (typical professional)
- **MPF**: Mandatory Provident Fund, 5% employer + 5% employee
- **Remote work**: Hybrid common post-COVID, but office-centric culture persists

## Social Dynamics
- **Population density**: 6,800/km² overall, 27,000/km² built-up
- **Gini coefficient**: 0.539 (high inequality)
- **Aging**: 21% 65+, fertility rate 0.77 (world lowest)
- **Mainland integration**: 150 daily quota (One Way Permit), tensions exist
- **Political climate**: Post-2020 NSL, reduced civil space, emigration wave 2020-2023

## Strengths
1. World's best public transit system
2. Unmatched hiking/nature access from urban core
3. Low tax regime (salaries tax max 15%)
4. Deep capital markets, financial career ceiling
5. Cantonese cultural distinctiveness
6. Healthcare outcomes #1 globally

## Weaknesses
1. Housing crisis: least affordable major market globally
2. Extreme density, small living spaces
3. Air quality impacted by Pearl River Delta + local emissions
4. Political uncertainty, emigration brain drain
5. Work culture intensity, long hours
6. Inequality, subdivided housing persistence
