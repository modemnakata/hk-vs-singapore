## Capital Markets

### Hong Kong: China's Global Capital Gateway
**Stock Exchange (HKEX)**
- **Listed Companies**: 2,600+ (Main Board + GEM)
- **Mainland Exposure**: H-shares (160+), Red Chips (100+), A-share access (Stock Connect)
- **Sectors**: Finance (25%), Properties (15%), Tech (12%), Consumer (10%), Industrials (10%)
- **Stock Connect**: Northbound (HK→Shanghai/Shenzhen), Southbound (Mainland→HK) — daily quota S$52B/NB, S$42B/SB
- **Derivatives**: HSI/HSCEI futures/options, stock futures, currency, commodities — #1 globally by volume
- **ETPs**: 180+ ETFs (A-share, thematic, crypto futures), L&I products

**Bond Market**
- **Size**: HK$2.5T+ outstanding
- **Government**: HKSAR Govt Bond (HK$100B+), Exchange Fund Bills/Notes (liquidity anchor)
- **Corporate**: Dim sum bonds (RMB offshore), green/sustainable bonds (HK$120B+ 2023)
- **RMB Hub**: Largest offshore RMB center (RMB 1T+ deposits), CIBM Direct, Bond Connect

**Fund Management**
- **AUM**: HK$30T (2023), 70% non-HK assets, 30% HK/China
- **Regime**: SFC Type 1/4/9, Open-Ended Fund Companies (OFC), LP regime, tax concession (8.25%)
- **China Access**: Mutual Recognition of Funds (MRF), QFII/RQFII, Wealth Management Connect
- **Family Offices**: 2,700+ (profits tax exemption s.14, 8.25% rate, no approval needed)

### Singapore: Asia's Wealth & Private Markets Hub
**Stock Exchange (SGX)**
- **Listed Companies**: 600+ (Mainboard + Catalist)
- **Sectors**: Finance (35%), REITs (20%), Industrials (15%), Telecom/Tech (10%), Consumer (10%)
- **REITs**: #1 in Asia ex-Japan (S$120B, 40+ REITs), S-REIT regime, tax transparency
- **Derivatives**: NKY, CNX, MSCI Asia, FX, commodities — growing but smaller than HK
- **Dual Listing**: HK-SG dual tranche (e.g., JD, Meituan, NIO secondary)

**Bond Market**
- **Size**: S$600B+ outstanding
- **Government**: SGS (S$400B), T-bills, Green SGS (inaugural 2022, S$2.4B)
- **Corporate**: Investment-grade dominant, sustainability-linked bonds growing
- **Asian Dollar Bonds**: Primary issuance hub (US$ denominated Asian credit)

**Fund Management**
- **AUM**: S$4.2T (2023), 85% non-SG assets
- **Regime**: MAS CMS license, VCC (Variable Capital Company) — 1,000+ VCCs since 2020
- **Tax**: Enhanced Tier Fund (13R), S13X (onshore), 13CA (non-resident) — 0-10% concessionary
- **Family Offices**: 1,500+ (13U/13X/13CA), S$1B+ AUM threshold for 13U, ONE Pass for principals
- **Private Markets**: PE/VC AUM US$200B+, #1 in SEA, GIC/Temasek anchor LPs

## Risk Factors

### Hong Kong Specific
1. **Geopolitical**: US-China decoupling, sanctions risk, NSL/Article 23 uncertainty
2. **China Property**: Contagion risk (Evergrande, Country Garden), local developer exposure
3. **Capital Flow**: Northbound volatility, RMB pressure, Fed policy import
4. **Demographics**: Aging, emigration, workforce shrinkage
5. **Property Overhang**: Vacancy, negative equity risk, rate sensitivity

### Singapore Specific
1. **Small Market**: Limited domestic demand, export-dependent, key man risk
2. **Rate Sensitivity**: Open economy, property/REIT leverage, household debt
3. **Competition**: Rising SEA cities (KL, Jakarta, HCMC, Bangkok) for talent/capital
4. **Climate**: Physical risk (sea level), transition risk (carbon tax, green mandates)
5. **Succession**: Political transition (post-Lee), policy continuity questions

## Allocation Framework

### For China-Bullish Investor
- **Hong Kong overweight**: Stock Connect (A-shares), H-share tech, property (selective), RMB bonds
- **Singapore complement**: Private markets (SEA exposure), wealth preservation, REITs

### For Global Diversifier
- **Singapore overweight**: Private equity, REITs, wealth structures, biomedical, green economy
- **Hong Kong satellite**: China access (Stock Connect), financials, offshore RMB

### For Yield Seeker
- **Singapore REITs**: 5-7% yield, growth, governance
- **HK REITs**: 4-5% yield, defensive (Link), limited growth
- **Both**: Investment-grade bonds, preferred securities

### For Family Office / UHNW
- **Singapore**: VCC, trust law, 13U/13X/13CA, ONE Pass, substance, stability
- **Hong Kong**: S.14 exemption, trust law (common law), proximity to Mainland family, GBA

## Bottom Line

**Hong Kong**: The China alpha engine — unparalleled access to Mainland capital, companies, consumers. High beta, policy-sensitive, cyclical. Best for: China conviction, financials, offshore RMB, GBA thematic.

**Singapore**: The compounding machine — stable governance, deep private markets, wealth preservation, innovation ecosystem. Lower beta, structural growth, high governance. Best for: Global allocation, private markets, REITs, family office, biomed, green economy.

**Optimal**: Not either/or. Hong Kong for China beta + financial alpha. Singapore for global beta + private markets alpha + wealth preservation. The sophisticated portfolio holds both.
