**Key HDB Rules**:
- **BTO**: Ballot, 3-5 year wait, 5-year MOP (Minimum Occupation Period)
- **Resale**: Immediate, CPF usage allowed, ethnic integration quota
- **Lease**: 99 years, no freehold, decay affects value after 60 years
- **CPF**: Can use OA for downpayment + monthly mortgage
- **Grants**: Up to S$80k (EHG) + S$30k (PHG) + S$20k (SHG) = S$130k max

## CPF (Central Provident Fund) — Forced Savings

### Contribution Rates (Monthly Wage Ceiling S$6,800)
| Age | Employer | Employee | Total | Allocation (OA/SA/MA) |
|-----|----------|----------|-------|----------------------|
| ≤55 | 17% | 20% | 37% | 62%/16%/22% |
| 55-60 | 14.5% | 15% | 29.5% | 51%/22%/27% |
| 60-65 | 11% | 9.5% | 20.5% | 34%/29%/37% |
| 65-70 | 9% | 7% | 16% | 21%/34%/45% |
| >70 | 7.5% | 5% | 12.5% | 11%/36%/53% |

### Accounts
- **OA (Ordinary)**: Housing, education, investment, insurance (2.5% interest)
- **SA (Special)**: Retirement, investment (4.0% interest)
- **MA (MediSave)**: Healthcare, insurance (4.0% interest)
- **RA (Retirement)**: Formed at 55 from OA+SA, payouts from 65

### Withdrawal
- **Age 55**: S$5,000 + amounts > Full Retirement Sum (FRS: S$205,800 in 2024)
- **Age 65**: Monthly payouts (CPF LIFE), min S$1,000-2,000/mo depending on RA

## Cost-Saving Strategies

1. **HDB BTO + grants**: Lowest housing cost, forced savings via CPF
2. **Hawker + cook**: S$500-700/mo food vs S$1,500+ dining out
3. **Public transport only**: Save S$20k-40k/yr vs car
4. **SRS (Supplementary Retirement Scheme)**: Tax deferral, invest proceeds
5. **MediSave for IP premiums**: No cash outlay for private coverage
6. **SkillsFuture credits**: S$500 base + top-ups for courses
7. **CDC vouchers**: S$300-500/yr (household, heartland merchants)
8. **GST voucher**: S$400-700/yr (lower income)
9. **Baby bonus + CDA**: S$11k-13k + S$3k-15k matched savings
10. **Credit card optimization**: 3-5% cashback on dining/groceries/transport
