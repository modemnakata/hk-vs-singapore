## Key Cost Drivers

### Hong Kong Expensive
1. **Housing**: World's least affordable, per sq ft highest
2. **Imported goods**: Most food/consumer goods imported
3. **Alcohol/tobacco**: High duties (though no VAT)
4. **Private education**: Intl schools HK$180-300k/year
5. **Domestic helpers**: HK$4,870/min wage + levy + insurance

### Singapore Expensive
1. **Car ownership**: COE ~S$90k (10-yr), +ARF, +road tax
2. **Alcohol**: Highest duties in region (S$88/L alcohol)
3. **Private healthcare**: IP riders + MediSave top-ups
4. **Education**: Intl schools S$30-50k/year
5. **GST**: 9% on everything (vs HK 0% VAT)
6. **Carbon tax**: S$25/tCO2e (rising to 50-80 by 2030)

### Hong Kong Cheaper
1. **Food**: Hawker-style, wet markets, intense competition
2. **Public transport**: MTR distance-based, highly subsidized
3. **Tax**: Salaries tax max 15%, no CGT, no VAT, no dividend tax
4. **Utilities**: Regulated, reasonable
5. **Mobile data**: Very competitive, 100GB+ for <HK$100

### Singapore Cheaper
1. **Housing (owned)**: HDB subsidized, CPF pays mortgage
2. **Hawker food**: S$3-6 complete meals, UNESCO heritage
3. **Domestic helpers**: Lower levy (S$60-300 vs HK$1,125)
4. **Groceries**: Better supply chains, NTUC FairPrice scale
5. **Childcare**: Subsidies up to S$1,040/mo (infant care)

**Bottom line**: Singaporeans earn ~60% more in PPP terms, but face higher absolute costs for discretionary items. Hong Kongers have lower nominal incomes but zero VAT and lower food/transport costs.

## Tax Impact on Disposable Income

**HK (Annual HK$1M income)**:
- Salaries tax: ~HK$125k (15% standard rate)
- MPF: HK$18k (capped)
- **Net: ~HK$857k (85.7%)**

**SG (Annual S$170k ≈ HK$1M income)**:
- Income tax: ~S$9,300 (progressive, ~5.5%)
- CPF employee: S$10,800 (capped, age <55)
- **Net: ~S$150k (88%)** + CPF savings (S$27k total w/ employer)

**Singapore wins on forced savings (CPF), Hong Kong wins on immediate cash flow.**

## Hidden Costs

### Hong Kong
- **Stamp duty**: 15% BSD (non-PR) + 15% SSD (flip <36m) on property
- **Rates**: Quarterly property tax ~5% of rateable value
- **Management fees**: HK$3-8/sq ft (luxury higher)
- **Club debentures**: HK$500k-5M+ for social/sports clubs
- **School debentures**: HK$300k-3M+ for priority admission

### Singapore
- **BSD**: 1-6% (progressive), ABSD 20-65% (foreigners/entities)
- **Property tax**: 0-32% (owner-occupied progressive, non-owner higher)
- **MCST fees**: S$300-1,000+/mo (condo maintenance)
- **COE renewal**: S$80-100k every 10 years (car)
- **Maid levy**: S$60 (concession) - S$300 (standard) monthly
