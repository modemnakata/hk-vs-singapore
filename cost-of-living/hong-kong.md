### Purchase Market
- **Median price**: HK$140k/sq ft (private), HK$55k/sq ft (subsidized sale)
- **Price-to-income**: 23.2x (world #1, Demographia 2024)
- **Downpayment**: 30% (first home), 40-50% (non-first), 50% (non-resident)
- **Stamp duties**:
  - BSD: 1.5-4.25% (progressive)
  - BSD (non-PR): +15% (total ~19-20%)
  - SSD: 15% (<6m), 10% (6-12m), 5% (12-36m)
- **Mortgage rates**: H+1.25% to H+2.0% (H = HIBOR ~5% 2024)
- **Stress test**: 2% above contracted rate

### Public Housing
- **PRH (rental)**: 800k units, avg rent HK$2,300, wait 5.6 years
- **HOS/Green Form**: Subsidized sale, 5-year resale restriction
- **Income/asset limits**: Tight, exclude most professionals

### Car Ownership (Rare for Expats)
- **First registration tax**: 40-115% of taxable value
- **Annual license**: HK$4,000-12,000 (by engine size)
- **Fuel**: ~HK$20-23/L (highest in region)
- **Parking**: HK$2,500-6,000/mo (residential), HK$40-60/hr (commercial)
- **Tunnel fees**: HK$8-50/trip
- **Total annual**: HK$100k-200k+ (excluding depreciation)

### Taxis/Rideshare
- **Taxi flagfall**: HK$27 (urban), 3,500m, then HK$1.9/200m
- **Uber**: ~1.5-2x taxi, UberX/Black/LUX
- **Monthly typical**: HK$500-2,000 (supplement transit)

## Taxation

### Salaries Tax (2024/25)
| Net Income | Rate |
|------------|------|
| First 50,000 | 2% |
| Next 50,000 | 6% |
| Next 50,000 | 10% |
| Next 50,000 | 14% |
| Remainder | 17% |
| **Standard rate option** | **15% on net (after deductions)** |

### Key Deductions/Allowances
- Basic: HK$132,000 (single), HK$264,000 (married)
- Child: HK$130,000 each (additional HK$130k year of birth)
- Dependent parent/grandparent: HK$55,000-60,000
- MPF: HK$18,000 cap
- VHIS: HK$8,000 per policy
- Home loan interest: HK$100,000
- Self-education: HK$100,000
- Charitable donations: 35% of income

### Other Taxes
- **Profits tax**: 8.25% first HK$2M, 16.5% thereafter (corporate)
- **Property tax**: 15% on net assessable value
- **Stamp duty**: As above (property, stock 0.13% each side)
- **No**: VAT/GST, capital gains, dividend, estate, gift tax

## Domestic Helper (Nearly Universal for Families)
- **Min wage**: HK$4,870/mo (2024)
- **Levy**: HK$1,125/year (employees' retraining)
- **Insurance**: HK$500-1,000/year
- **Food/accommodation**: In-kind (live-in mandatory)
- **Total monthly**: ~HK$6,500-7,500 all-in
- **Contract**: 2 years, standard contract, Labor Dept oversight

## Cost-Saving Strategies

1. **Live in New Territories**: 30-40% rent savings, 30-45 min commute
2. **Wet markets + cook**: 50% food savings vs dining out
3. **Public transport only**: No car saves HK$100k+/year
4. **VHIS + tax deduction**: Effective 15-20% premium reduction
5. **ESF vs private intl**: Save HK$50-100k/year per child
6. **MPF offset**: Use for mortgage (if eligible)
7. **Credit card rewards**: 2-5% cashback on dining/groceries/online
8. **Government subsidies**: Rates concession, electricity subsidy (periodic)
