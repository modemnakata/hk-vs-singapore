# Housing: Hong Kong Deep Dive

## Market Structure

### Tenure Types
| Type | Share | Characteristics |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| **Private Ownership** | ~50% | Leasehold (50-999 years), freehold rare (~5%), high management fees |
| **Public Rental Housing (PRH)** | ~30% | HA owned, income/asset tested, avg 5.6yr wait, rent ~HK$2,300/mo |
| **Subsidized Sale (HOS/GSH/TSPS)** | ~15% | 5-yr resale restriction, buyer must be eligible, discount to market |
| **Interim/Transitional** | ~5% | Village houses, staff quarters, temporary |

### Private Market Segments
| Segment | Price (psf) | Typical Buyer | Yield |
|---------|-------------|---------------|-------|
| **Ultra-Luxury (The Peak, Deep Water Bay)** | HK$300k-600k+ | UHNW, mainland, tycoons | 1.5-2.5% |
| **Luxury (Mid-Levels, Repulse Bay, Kowloon Tong)** | HK$180k-300k | HNW, expats, professionals | 2.0-3.0% |
| **Mass Market (New Territories, Kowloon East)** | HK$120k-180k | Local families, investors | 2.5-3.5% |
| **Micro/Subdivided** | HK$80k-120k (effective) | Low income, migrants | 4-6% (illegal) |

## Supply Dynamics

### Land System
- **All land leasehold** from Government (SAR), premium paid for development rights
- **Land Sale**: Public auction/tender (Government Land Sales Programme)
- **Redevelopment**: Private developers assemble sites, pay premium for plot ratio increase
- **Supply Target**: 301,000 units (2023-2033) — 131,000 public, 170,000 private
- **Actual Completions (2023)**: ~17,000 private, ~12,000 public

### Pipeline (2024-2028)
| Source | Estimated Units | Key Areas |
|--------|-----------------|-----------|
| **Government Land Sales** | 25,000-30,000 | Kai Tak, Tung Chung, Tseung Kwan O, Hung Shui Kiu |
| **Redevelopment (URA + private)** | 15,000-20,000 | Urban areas, old tenements |
| **Rail + Property (MTR)** | 10,000-15,000 | Station sites, depot tops |
| **Northern Metropolis** | 50,000+ (by 2033) | San Tin, Kwu Tung North, Hung Shui Kiu |
- **Lantau Tomorrow Vision**: 1,700ha reclamation, 260-400k units (2030s+) — **highly uncertain**

## Price Trends & Cycles

### Historical Cycles
| Cycle | Peak | Trough | Decline | Duration | Driver |
|-------|------|--------|---------|----------|--------|
| **1997 Asian Financial Crisis** | 1997 | 2003 | -65% | 6 years | Currency, oversupply, SARS |
| **2008 Global Financial Crisis** | 2008 | 2009 | -30% | 1 year | Lehman, credit crunch |
| **2019-2024 Current** | 2021 | 2024 (ongoing) | -20%+ | 3+ years | NSL, rates, China sentiment, emigration |

### Current Market (2024)
- **Price Index (R&VD)**: ~310 (1999=100), down from 395 peak (2021)
- **Transaction Volume**: ~4,000-5,000/mo (vs 10,000+ peak) — 30-year low
- **Unsold Inventory**: ~10,000 units (completed + presale) — developers holding
- **Mortgage Rates**: HIBOR + 1.25-2.0% (HIBOR ~5% 2024), stress test at +2%
- **LTV Limits**: 50% (non-first), 60% (first, ≤HK$10M), 70% (first, ≤HK$7M) — tightened 2023

## Buyer Segments & Behavior

### Local Owner-Occupiers (~60% of transactions)
- **Profile**: Dual-income families, upgrading from public/older private
- **Budget**: HK$8M-20M (mass), HK$20M-50M (mid), HK$50M+ (luxury)
- **Financing**: High LTV, parental gifts ("Bank of Mum and Dad" ~30% of downpayments)
- **Sentiment**: Cautious — wait for bottom, rate cuts, policy easing

### Mainland Buyers (~20-25% historically, <10% 2024)
- **Channels**: Home Return Permit (HRP) holders, corporate structures
- **Preferred**: Luxury (Mid-Levels, The Peak, Kowloon Station), new launches
- **Barriers**: 15% BSD (non-PR), capital controls (US$50k/yr), sentiment, travel
- **Impact**: Marginal price setter at top end; volume collapsed post-2019

### Investors (Local + Foreign) (~15-20%)
- **Local**: Yield-focused (2.5-3.5%), leverage, SSD avoidance (hold 36m+)
- **Foreign**: 15% BSD + 15% SSD = 30%+ upfront cost — negligible volume
- **REITs/Funds**: Link REIT (largest), Champion, Sunlight — institutional holders

### End-Users from Greater Bay Area (Emerging)
- **Shenzhen/HK Cross-Border Talent**: Buy in Northern Metropolis, commute via XRL/MTR
- **Budget**: HK$5M-15M, near border stations (Lok Ma Chau, Hung Shui Kiu)
- **Policy Support**: Talent schemes, mortgage recognition (cross-border income)

## Public Housing Deep Dive

### Public Rental Housing (PRH)
- **Stock**: ~800,000 units, 2.1M residents (28% population)
- **Average Rent**: HK$2,300/mo (income-based, 10-20% household income)
- **Wait Time**: 5.6 years (general), 3.5 years (elderly), 2.5 years (non-elderly singleton)
- **Allocation**: Quota system (elderly, family, singleton, non-elderly)
- **Quality**: improving (newer blocks 400-500sf, lifts, better layout)

### Subsidized Sale Flats (HOS/Green Form/White Form)
| Scheme | Target | Discount | Restriction |
|--------|--------|----------|-------------|
| **HOS** | General public (income ≤HK$60k/mo) | 30-50% | 5-yr MOP, then open market (premium payable) |
| **Green Form** | PRH tenants | 40-60% | 5-yr MOP, then open market |
| **White Form** | Non-PRH, higher income | 20-30% | 5-yr MOP, then open market |
| **Starter Homes** | Young professionals | Market-based | Pilot, limited supply |

- **Annual Supply**: ~8,000-12,000 units (combined)
- **Oversubscription**: 3-10x typical, ballot-based
- **Resale Market (HOS secondary)**: Active, premium to primary, no buyer restrictions after 5-yr MOP

## Rental Market

### Private Residential Rental
| District | 1BR Median | 2BR Median | 3BR Median | Yield |
|----------|------------|------------|------------|-------|
| **Mid-Levels/Central** | HK$25,000 | HK$38,000 | HK$55,000 | 2.5-3.0% |
| **Kowloon (TST, Ho Man Tin)** | HK$20,000 | HK$30,000 | HK$42,000 | 3.0-3.5% |
| **New Towns (Sha Tin, Tseung Kwan O, Tuen Mun)** | HK$15,000 | HK$22,000 | HK$30,000 | 3.0-3.5% |
| **Outlying (Discovery Bay, Tung Chung)** | HK$20,000 | HK$28,000 | HK$38,000 | 2.5-3.0% |

- **Lease Terms**: 2 years standard, 1 month deposit + 1 month advance
- **Commission**: 50% one month rent (split landlord/tenant)
- **Stamp Duty**: 0.25% (≤1yr), 0.5% (1-3yr), 1% (>3yr) — tenant usually pays
- **Vacancy**: ~3-4% (rising with emigration)

### Subdivided/Substandard Housing
- **Units**: ~110,000 (official), ~200,000+ (NGO estimate)
- **Residents**: ~220,000 (official), ~400,000+ (estimate)
- **Median Rent**: HK$4,500-6,500 for 50-100sf
- **Conditions**: Poor ventilation, fire risk, no tenure security
- **Policy**: Light Public Housing (LPH) — transitional, 30,000 units by 2027

## Mortgage & Financing

### Bank Mortgages
- **Pricing**: HIBOR + spread (1.25-2.0%) or Fixed (2.5-3.5% for 2-3yr)
- **Stress Test**: 2% above contracted rate (HKMA guideline)
- **DSR Cap**: 50% (net income), 40% (if variable rate)
- **Insurance**: Mortgage insurance (HKMC) for LTV >60% (first home) / >50% (non-first)
- **Refinancing**: Cash-out refi restricted, penalty periods (1-3yr)

### Developer Financing (First-hand)
- **Stage Payment**: 5% signing, 5% 30-60 days, 90% completion
- **Deferred Payment**: 10-20% deferred 2-3 years (marketing tool)
- **Mortgage Referral**: Developer-arranged (often higher rate)

## Risks & Challenges

### Structural
1. **Demographic Decline**: Population peaking 2024, aging, low fertility (0.77), emigration
2. **Lease Expiry Uncertainty**: 50-year leases (pre-1997) expiring 2047-2057 — no clarity on renewal terms
3. **GBA Competition**: Shenzhen (cheaper, newer), Qianhu (policy), Dongguan (industrial)
4. **Climate Risk**: Typhoon, flood, slope failure — insurance cost rising, some areas uninsurable

### Cyclical
1. **Interest Rates**: USD peg = imported Fed policy, high household debt (80% GDP)
2. **China Sentiment**: Property crisis, capital outflow restrictions, geopolitical risk
3. **Office-to-Resi Conversion**: Limited (zoning, floor plate, economics)
4. **Developer Distress**: Evergrande, Country Garden, Sunac — contagion to HK lenders

## Future Outlook

### Bull Case
- Fed rate cuts (2025+) → mortgage relief → demand recovery
- GBA integration accelerates → Northern Metropolis demand surge
- Talent schemes attract 100k+ high-income households
- Land supply constrained → price floor supported

### Bear Case
- China property crisis deepens → sentiment/credit contagion
- Emigration continues → 200k+ units vacant by 2030
- Lease expiry fear depresses pre-1997 stock permanently
- Climate events (super typhoon) → insurance withdrawal → value destruction

### Base Case
- **Prices**: Sideways to -5% (2024-2026), then track inflation + GDP
- **Volume**: Low (40-50k/yr) until rate clarity
- **Rents**: Resilient (yield support), 2-3% growth
- **Best Opportunities**: New Territories mass (yield + GBA), industrial/logistics (structural), REITs (liquid, yield)

## Actionable Advice

### For Owner-Occupier
- **Buy if**: 7+ year horizon, 30%+ downpayment, rate stress-tested, GBA commuter
- **Wait if**: Short horizon, high leverage, uncertain residency, luxury segment
- **Best Value**: 3BR in Sha Tin/Tseung Kwan O/Tuen Mun (MTR, schools, 3.5% yield)

### For Investor
- **Avoid**: High-leverage luxury, pre-1997 lease expiry, subdivided yield plays
- **Consider**: REITs (Link, Champion — 4-5% yield, liquid), industrial (logistics/data center), Northern Metropolis land banking (long horizon)
- **Structure**: SPV + mortgage interest deduction (if HK tax resident), stamp duty planning

### For Mainland Buyer
- **Wait**: BSD 15% + capital controls + sentiment = poor entry
- **Alternative**: Shenzhen Bay / Qianhai / Hengqin — closer, cheaper, policy support
- **If Must**: Corporate structure (tax efficiency), HRP holder, 10+ year hold

### For Tenant
- **Negotiate**: 5-10% below asking, 2-month deposit, furniture inclusion
- **Areas**: New Territories (30% discount, 35min commute), older Kowloon (character, value)
- **Rights**: Security of tenure (Landlord & Tenant Consolidation Ordinance), rent control (pre-1995 only)
