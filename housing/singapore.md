## HDB System Deep Dive

### BTO (Build-To-Order) Process
1. **Launch**: HDB announces sites (monthly/quarterly), indicates estimated prices
2. **Application**: 1-week window, Singpass login, income ceiling check
3. **Ballot**: Computerized, priority schemes (MCPS, PFSS, MFSS, etc.)
4. **Selection**: Queue number → choose unit (flat type, floor, block)
5. **Agreement**: Sign lease, pay 5-10% downpayment (CPF/cash)
6. **Construction**: 3-5 years (design-build), progress payments via CPF
7. **Key Collection**: Inspect, collect keys, start 5-year MOP

### Priority Schemes (Increase Ballot Chances)
| Scheme | Eligibility | Benefit |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| **MCPS** (Married Child Priority) | Married couple, child <16, living with/near parents | 2x ballot chances |
| **PFSS** (Parenthood Priority) | First-timer couple, citizen child <18 | 2x ballot chances |
| **MFSS** (Married First-Timer) | First-timer couple, married <2yr | 1.5x ballot chances |
| **FTSS** (First-Timer) | First-timer couple/family | Standard |
| **SSS** (Second-Timer) | Second-timer couple/family | Lower priority |
| **SHS** (Single) | Single citizen ≥35 | 2-room Flexi only |

### Grants (Stackable, Up to S$130k)
| Grant | Amount | Eligibility |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| **EHG** (Enhanced Housing Grant) | Up to S$80k | Income ≤S$9k, first-timer, flat type |
| **PHG** (Proximity Housing Grant) | S$20k-30k | Buy near parents/children (4km) |
| **SHG** (Singles Grant) | S$20k-35k | Single, income ≤S$7k, 2-room Flexi |
| **CPF Housing Grant (Family)** | Up to S$50k | Older scheme, being phased into EHG |

### CPF Usage for HDB
- **Downpayment**: 10-20% (CPF OA + cash)
- **Monthly Mortgage**: CPF OA (entire or partial)
- **Legal/Stamp Fees**: CPF OA
- **Retirement Protection**: Must set aside Basic Retirement Sum (BRS: S$102,900) before using OA for mortgage at 55

### Resale Market
- **Eligibility**: Citizens/PRs (SPR 3yr), income ceiling (S$14k/21k), ethnic quota
- **Process**: Option to Purchase (OTP) → 4% exercise fee → completion 8-12 weeks
- **CPF Usage**: Full OA for purchase + mortgage (subject to valuation limit)
- **Lease Decay**: Price declines accelerate after 60 years remaining
- **VERS/SELECTERS**: Future redevelopment schemes (untested, political promise)

## Key Risks

### Structural
1. **HDB Lease Decay**: 99-year clock — value → zero at expiry, VERS untested, SELECTERS limited
2. **Aging Population**: 25% >65 by 2030 → downsizing wave, HDB supply glut risk
3. **Land Scarcity**: 734km², reclamation costs rising, competing uses (defense, airport, reserves)
4. **Climate Adaptation**: Coastal protection S$100B+, insurance cost, property value at risk

### Policy
1. **ABSD Overhang**: Foreign demand permanently capped, luxury/CCR dependent on locals
2. **Supply Pipeline**: 50k+ units completing 2024-2026 (BTO + private + EC) — absorption risk
3. **Interest Rate Sensitivity**: High household leverage (mortgage/GDP ~40%), floating rate dominant
4. **CPF Adequacy**: OA depletion for housing → retirement shortfall, property monetization pressure

### Market
1. **Office/Retail Structural Shift**: WFH, e-commerce → commercial value decline → mixed-use conversion
2. **En-bloc Drought**: ABSD + high construction cost + rate → collective sales stalled
3. **Foreign Capital Rotation**: Singapore → Japan, Australia, UK, US (yield, currency, growth)

## Actionable Advice

### For Citizen First-Timer (Couple, Income S$10k-14k)
- **Best Path**: BTO (non-mature) + max grants (EHG S$80k + PHG S$30k) = S$110k subsidy
- **Timeline**: 3-5 years wait, 5-year MOP, then upgrade option
- **Alternative**: Resale 4-room (mature) — immediate, S$500k-650k, less grant

### For Citizen Upgrader (HDB → Condo)
- **Strategy**: Sell HDB (after MOP), use proceeds + CPF for condo downpayment
- **Timing**: HDB resale high (2024), condo prices plateauing — good window
- **Budget**: S$1.5M-2.5M (RCR/OCR), 25% downpayment (5% cash + 20% CPF/cash)

### For PR (Income S$12k-20k)
- **HDB**: Resale only (after 3yr SPR), S$500k-700k, no EHG/PHG
- **Condo**: OCR/RCR (S$1.2M-2M), ABSD 5% (1st), 30% (2nd) — calculate all-in
- **Landed**: Not eligible (SLA approval extremely rare)

### For Foreigner (Income >S$20k)
- **Rent**: Only rational option — ABSD 60% makes buying irrational
- **If Must Buy**: Commercial/industrial (ABSD not applicable), or S$2.5M GIP → PR → buy
- **Sentosa Cove**: Only residential option (foreigner-eligible), leasehold, low yield

### For Investor (Local/Global)
- **Avoid**: CCR luxury (ABSD, oversupply, yield compression), 2nd+ property (ABSD + LTV)
- **Consider**: 
  - **Industrial/Logistics** (JTC leasehold, 4-5% yield, growth, REIT-accessible)
  - **S-REITs** (diversified, 5-7% yield, liquid, professional mgmt)
  - **OCR Condo** (3BR, family rental, 3.5% yield, MRT access, en-bloc optionality)
  - **Land Banking** (GLS sites via collective sale — high risk, high reward, 10yr horizon)

## Lease Decay & The 99-Year Question

| 20 years | 30-45 | ~2.0% |
| <10 years | <20 | Accelerating |

### VERS (Voluntary Early Redevelopment Scheme)
- **Concept**: Government buys back flats early (70+ years), redevelops at higher density
- **Status**: Legislation passed (2022), pilot sites TBD, compensation formula unknown
- **Risk**: May not cover all estates, compensation may be below market, political

### SELECTERS (Selective En bloc Redevelopment Scheme)
- **Current**: 4-5 precincts/year, 60-70 years lease, compensation at market value
- **Limitation**: Small scale, political, not a universal solution

## Bottom Line

**Singapore Housing = Forced Savings Vehicle + Consumption Good + Policy Instrument**

- **For Citizens**: HDB = subsidized asset, CPF-funded, retirement nest egg (if monetized)
- **For PRs**: Resale HDB or private — calculate ABSD, CPF, lease decay carefully
- **For Foreigners**: Rent. Buying = 60-65% upfront tax, leasehold, no CPF, low yield
- **For Investors**: Industrial > REITs > OCR Condo > Landed > CCR Condo > HDB (investment-grade)

**The Lease Decay Reality**: Every HDB/condo buyer is a "tenant with prepaid rent." The only questions: how fast does value erode, and will VERS bail you out? Price in 1%/yr depreciation after year 40.
