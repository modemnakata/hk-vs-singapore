## Regulatory Philosophy

### Hong Kong: Light-Touch, Market-Led, Common Law
- **Principle**: "Small government, big market" — minimal intervention, maximum transparency
- **Regulators**: Sectoral (SFC, HKMA, IA, OFCA, CEDB) — independent, industry-consulted
- **Legislation**: Ordinances (LegCo), common law gap-filling, judicial review available
- **China Factor**: National Security Law (2020) + Article 23 (2024) — new compliance layer, data/extradition risks
- **GBA Integration**: Rules harmonization (finance, data, professional services), "one country, two systems" leverage

### Singapore: Proactive, Whole-of-Government, Statutory
- **Principle**: Anticipatory regulation — sandbox, guidance, then legislate; "smart regulation"
- **Regulators**: MAS (finance), IMDA (infocomm/media), EDB (investment), ACRA (corporate) — coordinated
- **Legislation**: Acts (Parliament), subsidiary legislation, judicial deference to policy
- **International Standards**: Early adopter (Basel III, FATF, BEPS, GDPR-aligned PDPA)
- **ASEAN Hub**: Regulatory alignment (ACRA, ASEAN QR, cross-border payments, digital economy agreements)

## Company Incorporation & Maintenance

### Hong Kong
| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Entity Types** | Private Ltd (most common), Public Ltd, Branch, Rep Office, LP/LLP |
| **Incorporation** | 24-48 hrs (e-Registry), 1 director (natural person), 1 shareholder, 1 company secretary (HK resident/TCSP) |
| **Capital** | No minimum, no authorized capital concept, bearer shares abolished |
| **Registered Address** | Physical HK address (not PO Box) |
| **Annual Compliance** | Annual Return (NAR1), Audited FS (all companies), AGM (9 mo FYE), BIR BR renewal |
| **Significant Controllers Register** | Mandatory (SCR), accessible to law enforcement |
| **Beneficial Ownership** | AMLO regime, trust/nominee look-through |
| **Cost (Year 1)** | ~HK$10k-20k (incorp + secretary + address + BR) |
| **Cost (Annual)** | ~HK$8k-15k (secretary + audit + BR + filing) |

### Singapore
| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Entity Types** | Pte Ltd (most common), Public, Branch, Rep Office, LLP, LP, VCC |
| **Incorporation** | 1-2 days (BizFile+), 1 resident director (citizen/PR/EP), 1 shareholder, 1 company secretary (resident) |
| **Capital** | No minimum paid-up (S$1), no par value |
| **Registered Address** | Physical SG address (not PO Box), residential allowed (HDB/URA approval) |
| **Annual Compliance** | Annual Return (AR), AGM (6 mo FYE), Audited FS (unless small co exemption), Tax filing (ECI + Form C-S/C) |
| **Register of Controllers** | Mandatory (RORC), lodged with ACRA |
| **Beneficial Ownership** | AML/CTF regime, nominee disclosure, VCC register (private) |
| **Cost (Year 1)** | ~S$3k-8k (incorp + secretary + address + nominee director if needed) |
| **Cost (Annual)** | ~S$2k-5k (secretary + audit (if needed) + filing) |

## Key Sector Regulations

### Financial Services
| Aspect | Hong Kong (SFC/HKMA) | Singapore (MAS) |
|--------|---------------------|-----------------|
| **Banking License** | HKMA, 3-tier (licensed/restricted/deposit-taking) | MAS, full/wholesale/merchant |
| **Virtual Banks** | 8 licensed (2019-), digital-only | 4 digital bank licenses (2020) |
| **Asset Management** | SFC Type 9, ~HK$30T AUM, tax concession (8.25%) | MAS CMS license, ~S$4T AUM, VCC structure |
| **FinTech Sandbox** | HKMA FinTech Supervisory Sandbox | MAS FinTech Regulatory Sandbox + Sandbox Express |
| **Crypto/VASP** | SFC licensing (2023), professional investors only | MAS PSA license, DPT services, stablecoin framework |
| **Green Finance** | Green taxonomy, green bond grant, GBA green finance | Singapore-Asia taxonomy, transition finance, carbon services |

### Technology & Data
| Aspect | Hong Kong | Singapore |
|--------|-----------|-----------|

## Incentives & Support

### Hong Kong
- **Profits Tax Concessions**: 8.25% first HK$2M, 100% capex deduction (plant/machinery), R&D super-deduction (300% first S$2M, 200% thereafter)
- **Re-industrialization**: Land at premium, tax concessions, funding (RTTP, TSSSU, ITF)
- **Green Finance**: Green bond grant (HK$2.5M), taxonomy, transition finance
- **Talent**: Top Talent Pass (A/B/C), IANG (non-local grads), TechTAS (R&D)
- **GBA**: Wealth Management Connect, Cross-boundary Wealth Management Connect, data flow

### Singapore
- **Tax Incentives**: Pioneer Certificate (5-15% tax, 5-10yr), DIP (10% tax, 5yr), IP Development Incentive (5-10%)
- **R&D**: 250% tax deduction (first S$400M), RIE2025 funding (S$25B), open innovation
- **Enterprise**: EDB grants (training, productivity, internationalization), Startup SG (equity, grants, loans)
- **Sustainability**: Carbon tax credits, Green Finance grants, Enterprise Sustainability Programme
- **Talent**: Tech.Pass, ONE Pass (top talent), COMPASS (EP framework), SkillsFuture

## Bottom Line

| Choose Hong Kong If | Choose Singapore If |
|---------------------|---------------------|
| China/GBA market access primary | Global/SEA market access primary |
| Common law certainty (historical) | Regulatory predictability, proactive govt |
| Finance/legal/professional services | Tech/biomed/advanced manufacturing |
| Low tax, simple regime | Incentive-rich, grant-heavy ecosystem |
| Talent with China expertise | Global talent, structured immigration |
| Light-touch regulation | Clear guidelines, sandboxes, standards |
| Arbitration (HKIAC) | Arbitration (SIAC) + SICC + mediation |

**The China Variable**: HK offers direct GBA access but rising regulatory convergence risk. SG offers China connectivity without political integration risk, but at one remove.
