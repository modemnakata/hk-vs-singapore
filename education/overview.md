## System Architecture

### Hong Kong: 6+3+3+4, HKDSE, High Stakes
```
Kindergarten (3yr, K1-K3) — Private/NGO, voucher scheme (HK$30k/yr)
    │
Primary (6yr, P1-P6) — Government/Aided/DSS/Private
    ├── Local curriculum (Chinese medium + English subject)
    ├── EMI schools (English medium, ~110 secondary)
    └── DSS (Direct Subsidy) — Semi-private, own curriculum
    │
Secondary (6yr, S1-S6) — S1-3 junior, S4-6 senior
    ├── Banding (by P6 assessment) → School allocation
    ├── HKDSE (S6) — 4 core + 2-3 electives
    │   ├── Core: Chinese, English, Math, Citizenship & Social Development
    │   └── Electives: Sciences, Humanities, Arts, Applied Learning
    └── JUPAS → University admission (8 UGC-funded + self-financing)
    │
Tertiary
    ├── UGC-funded (8): HKU, CUHK, HKUST, PolyU, CityU, HKBU, LU, EdUHK
    ├── Self-financing (degree + sub-degree)
    └── VTC (Vocational Training Council) — Diploma/Higher Diploma
```

### Singapore: 6+4/5+2/3, PSLE → Streaming (Phasing Out) → National Exams
```
Preschool (K1-K2) — Anchor/Partner operators, MOE Kindergartens
    │
Primary (6yr, P1-P6) — All government/aided
    ├── Subject-based banding (from P3)
    ├── PSLE (P6) — AL scoring (Achievement Levels 1-8)
    └── Secondary posting by PSLE score + choice
    │
Secondary (4-5yr) — Full Subject-Based Banding (2024 full implementation)
    ├── G1/G2/G3 subjects (replaces Express/NA/NT streams)
    ├── Singapore-Cambridge SEC (G3) / N-Level (G1/G2)
    └── Post-sec pathways:
        ├── JC/MI (2-3yr) → A-Level / IB
        ├── Polytechnic (3yr) → Diploma
        ├── ITE (2-3yr) → Nitec/Higher Nitec
        └── Arts institutions (NAFA, LASALLE)
    │
Tertiary
    ├── Autonomous Unis (6): NUS, NTU, SMU, SUTD, SUSS, SIT
    ├── Polytechnics (5) — Applied degrees
    ├── ITE (3 colleges) — Technical skills
    └── Private degree partners (Kaplan, PSB, MDIS, etc.)
```

## Language & Medium of Instruction

### Hong Kong
| Level | Medium | Notes |
|-------|--------|-------|
| Kindergarten | Cantonese + English + Mandarin | Trilingual exposure |
| Primary (Aided/Gov) | Cantonese (Chinese subject: Putonghua option) | "Biliterate, Trilingual" goal |

| Secondary (CMI) | Cantonese (Chinese + Putonghua) | Majority |
| International | English (+ Mandarin/French/etc.) | IB/UK/US curricula |

**Tension**: Parents prefer EMI; policy pushes CMI + Putonghua. "Medium of instruction" = school choice proxy.

### Singapore
| Level | Medium | Notes |
|-------|--------|-------|
| Preschool | English + Mother Tongue | MT: Mandarin/Malay/Tamil |
| Primary | English (all subjects) + Mother Tongue | MT 30-40% curriculum time |
| Secondary | English + Mother Tongue | Higher MT for capable |
| JC/Poly | English | MT optional (H1/H2/H3) |
| International | English (+ Mandarin/others) | IB/UK/US/Aus/Indian curricula |

**Policy**: English = working language, Mother Tongue = cultural anchor. Bilingualism = national survival strategy.

## Curriculum & Assessment

### Hong Kong HKDSE (Since 2012)
- **4 Core**: Chinese, English, Mathematics, Citizenship & Social Development (replaced Liberal Studies 2021)
- **2-3 Electives**: From 20 subjects (Physics, Chem, Bio, Economics, History, Geography, BAFS, ICT, etc.)
- **Grading**: 5** (top) → 5* → 5 → 4 → 3 → 2 → 1 → U
- **University entry**: 33222 (3 core L3 + 2 electives L2) minimum, competitive programs 5*5*5*
- **International recognition**: UCAS tariff, accepted by UK/US/Aus/Canada unis

### Singapore A-Level / Poly / IB
- **A-Level (JC)**: H1/H2/H3 subjects, 3 H2 + 1 H1 + GP + PW, rank points (90 max)
- **Polytechnic**: 3yr diploma, GPA-based, 30%+ to uni
- **IB**: Offered at ACS(I), SJI, SOTA, St. Joseph's, UWCSEA — world-leading results (avg 40+)
- **University entry**: A-Level RP, Poly GPA, IB score, portfolio (aptitude-based)

## Expat Decision Factors

### Choose Hong Kong International If:
- **US curriculum preferred** (HKIS, AISHK, HKAC)
- **Cantonese environment** desired (cultural immersion)
- **China access** for family visits / business
- **Debenture as asset** (resale market exists, some appreciate)
- **ESF pathway** (subsidized, strong community, HK residency helps)

### Choose Singapore International If:
- **IB curriculum priority** (UWCSEA, CIS, Tanglin, OWIS — IB continuum)
- **English-first daily life** (easier transition, no language barrier)
- **Lower upfront cost** (no debenture, fees 20-30% lower)
- **Diverse peer group** (50+ nationalities typical)
- **Pathway to local uni** (IB → NUS/NTU/SMU well-trodden)

## Lifelong Learning & Upskilling

### Hong Kong
- **Employees Retraining Board (ERB)**: 700+ courses, placement-tied, levy-funded
- **Continuing Education Fund (CEF)**: HK$25k lifetime (reimbursable)
- **Qualifications Framework (QF)**: 7 levels, industry-recognized
- **VTC**: 50k+ students/yr, apprenticeship, earn & learn
- **University continuing ed**: HKU SPACE, CUHK CUSCS, HKUST, etc.

### Singapore
- **SkillsFuture**: S$500 base credit (25+), S$4k mid-career top-up (40+), S$4k career transition
- **Workforce Skills Qualifications (WSQ)**: National competency framework
- **Poly/ITE CET**: Part-time diplomas, certs, stackable
- **SUSS/SIT**: Adult-friendly, modular, work-study
- **Company training**: Wage Credit, Jobs Growth Incentive, SkillsFuture Enterprise Credit
