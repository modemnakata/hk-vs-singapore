# Immigration & Residency: Overview

## High-Level Comparison

| Metric | Hong Kong | Singapore | Advantage |
|--------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| **Permanent Residency Path** | 7 years ordinary residence | 2-6 years (EP/SPass/PR schemes) | Singapore |
| **Citizenship Path** | PR + 7 years + naturalization | PR + 2-6 years + registration | Singapore |
| **Dual Citizenship** | Not recognized (but tolerated) | Not allowed (must renounce) | Hong Kong |
| **Investment Migration** | CIES suspended (2023) | GIP (Global Investor Programme) | Singapore |
| **Talent Schemes** | Top Talent Pass, QMAS, IANG, TechTAS | EP, ONE Pass, Tech.Pass, COMPASS | Both strong |
| **Dependent Rights** | Work/study freely | Work (LOC), study (local fees) | Hong Kong |
| **Processing Time** | 4-8 weeks (most visas) | 2-8 weeks (most passes) | Singapore |
| **Certainty** | Declining (policy shifts) | High (transparent criteria) | Singapore |

## Visa & Work Pass Categories

### Hong Kong

#### General Employment Policy (GEP)
- **Eligibility**: Degree + relevant experience + salary market rate + sponsor
- **Duration**: 2-2-3 years (renewable), path to PR at 7 years
- **Dependents**: Spouse/children <18 → dependant visa (work/study freely)
- **Processing**: 4-6 weeks
- **No quota**, no labour market test

#### Top Talent Pass Scheme (TTPS) — Since 2022
| Category | Criteria | Duration | Renewal |
|----------|----------|----------|---------|
| **A** | Annual income ≥HK$2.5M (last year) | 2 years | 3 years (income ≥HK$2M) |
| **B** | Bachelor's from top 100 uni + 3yr exp (last 5yr) | 2 years | 3 years (employed) |
| **C** | Bachelor's from top 100 uni (last 5yr) <3yr exp | 2 years | 3 years (employed) — annual quota |
- **Dependents**: Same as GEP
- **PR Path**: 7 years ordinary residence

#### Quality Migrant Admission Scheme (QMAS)
- **Points-based**: Achievement (80/245) or General (100/245) test
- **Factors**: Age, education, experience, language, family
- **Quota**: Annual (reopened 2023, 4,000/yr)
- **Duration**: 1 year → renewal → PR at 7 years
- **No sponsor needed** initially

#### Immigration Arrangements for Non-local Graduates (IANG)
- **Eligibility**: Non-local grad from HK uni (full-time, local award)
- **Duration**: 12 months (job search) → GEP conversion
- **No sponsor needed** for first 12 months
- **Dependents**: Eligible

#### Technology Talent Admission Scheme (TechTAS)
- **Eligibility**: STEM talent, employed by HKSTP/Cyberport companies
- **Duration**: 2-2-3 years, fast-track (2 weeks)
- **PR Path**: 7 years

#### Capital Investment Entrant Scheme (CIES) — **Suspended 2023**
- Was: HK$30M investment (financial assets) → residency → PR at 7 years
- **Status**: Suspended pending review; no new applications

### Singapore

#### Employment Pass (EP) — Professionals
| Tier | Min Salary | Criteria | Duration |
|------|------------|----------|----------|
| **Standard** | S$5,000 (S$5,500 financial) | Degree + experience, COMPASS ≥40 pts | 2 years (renew 3) |
| **Young Grads** | S$4,500 | Top uni, COMPASS ≥40 pts | 2 years |
- **COMPASS** (since 2023): Points for salary, qualifications, diversity, local jobs, skills bonus, strategic bonus
- **Dependents**: Spouse/children <21 → Dependant's Pass (DP) — work via LOC
- **PR Path**: Eligible after 6 months (typically 2-3 years)

#### S Pass — Mid-Skilled
- **Min Salary**: S$3,150 (S$3,650 financial)
- **Criteria**: Diploma/degree + experience, quota (10-15% workforce), levy
- **Duration**: 2 years (renew 3)
- **Dependents**: Spouse/children <21 → DP (if S$6,000+ salary)

#### Work Permit — Sector-Specific
- **Sectors**: Construction, Marine, Manufacturing, Services, Confinement Nanny
- **Quota + Levy**: By sector, source country, skill level
- **No dependents** (except confinement nanny)

#### ONE Pass (Overseas Networks & Expertise) — Since 2022
| Category | Criteria | Duration |
|----------|----------|----------|
| **A** | Last drawn ≥S$30k/mo (or equivalent) | 5 years |
| **B** | Top talent (sports, arts, science, academia) | 5 years |
- **No sponsor needed**, multiple employers allowed
- **Dependents**: DP + LOC (work freely)
- **PR Path**: Eligible immediately

#### Tech.Pass — Since 2021
- **Criteria**: Established tech entrepreneur/leader/investor
- **Salary/Equity**: Last drawn ≥S$20k/mo OR led US$10M+ funding/valuation
- **Duration**: 2 years (renewable if criteria met)
- **Activities**: Start business, invest, mentor, sit on boards
- **Dependents**: DP + LOC

#### EntrePass — Entrepreneurs
- **Criteria**: Innovative business, funding/IP/incubator, 30% local hiring
- **Duration**: 1 year (renew 2 years if milestones met)
- **PR Path**: Eligible after 2-3 years

#### Global Investor Programme (GIP)
| Option | Investment | Requirements |
|--------|------------|--------------|
| **Option A** | S$2.5M in new/existing business | 3-yr business plan, 5 local jobs |
| **Option B** | S$2.5M in GIP fund | Fund invests in SG-based companies |
| **Option C** | S$2.5M in single family office | S$200M+ AUM, 2 investment professionals |
- **PR**: Direct PR for applicant + spouse + children <21
- **Processing**: 6-12 months

#### Permanent Residency (PR) — Common Pathways
| Scheme | Eligibility | Typical Timeline |
|--------|-------------|------------------|
| **PTS (Professionals/Technical/Skilled)** | EP/S Pass holders, 6+ months | 2-4 years |
| **GIP** | Investors | Immediate |
| **Family Ties** | SC spouse/child, aged parents | 1-2 years |
| **Students** | Studied 2+ years, national exam | 1-2 years |
| **Arts/Sports** | National representation | Case-by-case |

## Permanent Residency Comparison

### Hong Kong PR (Right of Abode / Right to Land)
| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Eligibility** | 7 years "ordinary residence" (continuous, not broken >6 months) |
| **Categories** | Chinese citizens (automatic), Non-Chinese (7-yr ordinary residence) |
| **Rights** | Live/work freely, vote (District Council), public housing (after 3-yr wait), public healthcare rates |
| **Obligations** | None (no NS, no tax difference) |
| **Loss** | Absent >36 months (Right to Land), deportation (crimes) |
| **Children** | Born in HK to PR parent → automatic PR (Chinese) / Right to Land (non-Chinese) |

### Singapore PR
| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Eligibility** | PTS (EP/S Pass 2-3 yrs), Family, GIP, Students, Arts/Sports |
| **Rights** | Live/work freely, CPF (full rates), HDB (after 3-yr SPR), public healthcare subsidies, education (local fees) |
| **Obligations** | **NS liability** (male PRs, 2nd gen), tax resident, CPF contributions |
| **Loss** | Absent >5 years (Re-Entry Permit expiry), renunciation, deportation |
| **Children** | Born to PR → citizen by registration (if one parent SC), else PR |

## Citizenship Comparison

### Hong Kong (Naturalization as Chinese National)
| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Eligibility** | PR + 7 years ordinary residence + "settled" intent + good character + Chinese language |
| **Process** | Application → Security clearance → Oath → Naturalization cert |
| **Dual Citizenship** | **Not recognized** — must declare change, but HK doesn't actively strip; China doesn't recognize dual |
| **Rights** | HKSAR Passport (visa-free 170+), Right of Abode, vote (LegCo), public office |
| **Timeline** | 12-24 months |

### Singapore (Registration)
| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Eligibility** | PR 2-6 years + employed/self-employed + good character + integration (NS for males) |
| **Process** | Application → Interview → Approval → Renunciation → Registration → Oath |
| **Dual Citizenship** | **Not allowed** — must renounce prior citizenship (except minors <21, defer to 22) |
| **Rights** | Singapore Passport (visa-free 190+, #1 globally), vote, public office, full subsidies |
| **Timeline** | 6-12 months |

## Dependent & Family Rights

| Right | Hong Kong (Dependant Visa) | Singapore (Dependant's Pass) |
|-------|----------------------------|------------------------------|
| **Work** | Freely (no separate permit) | Letter of Consent (LOC) — employer applies |
| **Study** | Local schools (free), international | Local schools (PR fees after 3yr SPR), international |
| **Healthcare** | Public (subsidized if PR, else full fee) | Public (PR rates after SPR) |
| **Banking/Driving** | Full access | Full access |
| **Parents** | Dependant visa (if >60, reliant) | Long-Term Visit Pass (LTVP) — no work |

## Student Visas

| Aspect | Hong Kong | Singapore |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| **Visa Type** | Student Visa (full-time, local award) | Student's Pass (full-time, approved institute) |
| **Work Rights** | Part-time (on-campus, 20hrs/wk), internship (course-related) | Part-time (16hrs/wk term, full vacation), internship |
| **Post-Grad** | IANG (12 months job search) | Job search (1 year, non-renewable) or EP/EntrePass |
| **PR Path** | IANG → GEP → 7yr PR | Student's Pass → EP → 2-4yr PR |

## Processing & Certainty

### Hong Kong
- **Immigration Department (ImmD)**: Professional, rule-based
- **Policy Risk**: NSL/Art 23 — security screening enhanced, political vetting alleged
- **Appeal**: Limited (judicial review only on procedural grounds)
- **Trends**: Tightening — more docs, longer processing, higher rejection for sensitive sectors

### Singapore
- **ICA / MOM**: Transparent criteria, self-assessment tools (COMPASS, EP eligibility)
- **Policy Risk**: Low — changes announced, phased, industry consulted
- **Appeal**: Structured (reconsideration, Minister appeal)
- **Trends**: Calibrating — COMPASS, fair consideration, quality over quantity

## Strategic Considerations

### For the Career Professional
- **Singapore**: Clearer PR path (2-4 yrs), CPF wealth building, citizenship option (if willing to renounce)
- **Hong Kong**: 7-year clock, no CPF equivalent, citizenship = Chinese nationality (complex)

### For the Entrepreneur
- **Singapore**: GIP (direct PR), EntrePass, Tech.Pass, ONE Pass — multiple pathways, grant ecosystem
- **Hong Kong**: Investment scheme suspended, TechTAS (niche), GEP — fewer dedicated paths

### For the Family
- **Singapore**: Better healthcare/education subsidies for PR, NS liability for sons (factor in)
- **Hong Kong**: No NS, but full-cost healthcare/education until PR (3-yr wait for HDB)

### For the Investor/HNW
- **Singapore**: GIP (S$2.5M → PR), family office (13U/13X/13CA), trust law, stability
- **Hong Kong**: CIES suspended, family office (s.14), trust law, China access

### For the Digital Nomad / Remote Worker
- **Neither has a true digital nomad visa**
- **Hong Kong**: Visitor (90-180 days), extendable, no work rights
- **Singapore**: Visitor (30-90 days), extendable, no work rights — but Tech.Pass/ONE Pass if qualified

## The "China Factor"

| Aspect | Hong Kong | Singapore |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| **Mainland Talent** | Mainland Talent & Professionals Scheme (no quota, 2-2-3) | EP (COMPASS), PRC nationals significant cohort |
| **Home Return Permit** | HK PR → HRP (mainland travel/residence) | N/A |
| **Taiwan Talent** | Special scheme (Taiwanese professionals) | EP (same as others) |
| **GBA Mobility** | HK PR + HRP → live/work in 9 GBA cities | No direct scheme |

## Bottom Line

| Profile | Better Choice | Why |
|---------|---------------|-----|
| **Young professional, 5-10 yr horizon** | Singapore | Faster PR, CPF, citizenship option, certainty |
| **Mid-career, China-focused** | Hong Kong | GEP + Mainland scheme, HRP, GBA access |
| **Entrepreneur/Founder** | Singapore | GIP, EntrePass, Tech.Pass, grants, ecosystem |
| **HNW / Family Office** | Singapore | GIP, 13U/13X, trust law, stability, passport power |
| **Dual citizen tolerant** | Hong Kong | De facto tolerated, no renunciation required |
| **Male with sons** | Hong Kong | No NS liability |
| **Top global talent (income >US$300k)** | Either | TTPS Cat A / ONE Pass Cat A — both 5-yr, flexible |
| **Recent grad from top uni** | Hong Kong (IANG) / Singapore (EP) | IANG: 12-mo job search no sponsor; EP: higher salary floor |

**Singapore wins on**: Speed to PR, citizenship pathway, system certainty, dependent work rights (LOC), wealth integration (CPF).

**Hong Kong wins on**: No NS, dual citizenship tolerance, China/GBA mobility (HRP), simpler dependent rights, 7-year clock is predictable (if you can wait).

**Critical Variable**: Willingness to renounce original citizenship for Singapore passport (top 3 globally). If yes → Singapore. If no → Hong Kong (Right of Abode) or stay on PR/visa indefinitely.
