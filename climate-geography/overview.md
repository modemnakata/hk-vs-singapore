## Geography & Topography

### Hong Kong: Vertical City, Rugged Landscape
**Land Composition**
- **Total**: 1,114 km² (Hong Kong Island 80, Kowloon 47, New Territories 977+)
- **Reclaimed**: ~70 km² (6.5%) — Central, Wan Chai, Causeway Bay, West Kowloon, HKIA, Tung Chung
- **Built-up**: ~25% (250 km²) — extremely dense, vertical
- **Country Parks**: 24 parks, 443 km² (40%) — protected, mountainous
- **Reservoirs**: 17 — 586M m³ capacity (Dongjiang import 70-80% supply)
- **Coastline**: 733 km — deep harbor (Victoria Harbour), 260+ outlying islands

**Topography**
- **Highest**: Tai Mo Shan (957m), Lantau Peak (934m), Sunset Peak (869m)
- **Terrain**: Steep ridges, deep valleys, limited flat land (coastal plains, valley floors)
- **Geology**: Volcanic (Lantau, Sai Kung), Granitic (HK Island, Kowloon), Sedimentary (NW NT)
- **Land Supply**: Extreme scarcity — country parks protected, brownfield (1,200 ha), reclamation (Lantau Tomorrow 1,700 ha)

**Urban Form**
- **Polycentric**: Central (CBD), Kowloon (TST/Mong Kok), New Towns (Tsuen Wan, Sha Tin, Tseung Kwan O, Tin Shui Wai, Yuen Long)
- **Transit-Oriented**: MTR stations = development nodes, "Rail + Property" model
- **Verticality**: World's most skyscrapers (>300m: 55+, >150m: 500+), high-rise residential norm

### Singapore: Planned Island, Managed Landscape
**Land Composition**
- **Total**: 734 km² (2024) — grew 25% since 1965 (581 km²) via reclamation
- **Reclaimed**: ~150 km² (20%) — Changi, Jurong, Tuas, Marina Bay, Sentosa, Punggol
- **Built-up**: ~30% — planned, zoned, lower density than HK
- **Nature Reserves**: 4 (Bukit Timah, Central Catchment, Labrador, Sungei Buloh) — 31 km² (4.2%)
- **Parks & Park Connectors**: 350+ parks, 360km connectors — "City in Nature" target
- **Reservoirs**: 17 — catchment 2/3 land area, NEWater (recycled), desalination
- **Coastline**: 193 km (mainland) + islands — heavily modified, seawalls

**Topography**
- **Highest**: Bukit Timah Hill (164m) — low, gentle ridges
- **Terrain**: Undulating lowlands, central catchment, drained swamps (Kallang, Jurong)
- **Geology**: Sedimentary (Bukit Timah Granite, Jurong Formation), alluvial, marine clay
- **Land Supply**: Managed via Master Plan, GLS, underground (caverns, Jurong Rock Caverns), reclamation (Long Island, "Long Island" concept)

**Urban Form**
- **Polycentric (planned)**: CBD (Raffles Place/Marina Bay), Regional Centres (Jurong Lake, Woodlands, Punggol, Tampines), Town Centres (HDB new towns)
- **Transit-Oriented**: MRT stations + bus interchanges = town centres, integrated developments
- **Height Limits**: 280m (CBD), lower elsewhere — airspace (Changi, Paya Lebar), no skyscraper race

## Climate Detail

### Hong Kong: Subtropical with Seasons
| Season | Months | Temp Range | Rainfall | Characteristics |
|--------|--------|------------|----------|-----------------|
| **Winter** | Dec-Feb | 10-20°C | Low (30-50mm/mo) | Dry, cool, sunny, northerly winds, occasional cold surges (<5°C) |
| **Spring** | Mar-May | 18-27°C | Rising (70-150mm/mo) | Humid, fog (Mar-Apr), thunderstorms, "Black Rain" warnings |
| **Summer** | Jun-Aug | 26-33°C | Peak (300-450mm/mo) | Hot, humid, typhoon season, SW monsoon, heat stress (HI >40°C) |
| **Autumn** | Sep-Nov | 20-28°C | Falling (100-200mm/mo) | Pleasant, lower humidity, typhoon tail risk, best visiting weather |

**Extremes**
- **Hottest**: 36.6°C (Aug 2022, urban), 39.0°C (Aug 2023, Sheung Shui)
- **Coldest**: -6.0°C (Jan 1893, Tai Mo Shan), 0.0°C (Jan 2016, Tai Mo Shan)
- **Wettest Day**: 534mm (Jul 2024, black rainstorm)
- **Typhoons**: Avg 6/yr in SCS, 1-2 direct hits, signal 8+ ~1.5/yr

### Singapore: Tropical Consistency
| Period | Months | Temp Range | Rainfall | Characteristics |
|--------|--------|------------|----------|-----------------|
| **Northeast Monsoon** | Dec-Mar | 24-31°C | High (250-300mm/mo) | Wet, windy (NE), cool surges, monsoon surges (Jan-Feb) |
| **Inter-Monsoon** | Apr-May, Oct-Nov | 25-32°C | Moderate (150-200mm/mo) | Thunderstorms (afternoon), light winds, hot |
| **Southwest Monsoon** | Jun-Sep | 25-32°C | Moderate (150-180mm/mo) | Drier, hazy (Sumatra fires), SW winds, "Sumatra squalls" |

**No Seasons** — minimal temp variation (24-32°C daily), consistent humidity (80-90%), daily thunderstorms (40% days)

**Extremes**
- **Hottest**: 37.0°C (Apr 1983, May 2023, May 2024)
- **Coldest**: 19.4°C (Jan 1934), 20.2°C (Jan 2018)
- **Wettest Day**: 512mm (Dec 1978), 240mm/hr (Apr 2024)
- **Haze**: PSI >100 (unhealthy) — 2013, 2015, 2019, 2023 (transboundary)

## Urban Planning Philosophy

### Hong Kong: Market-Led, Density-Driven, Conservation-Constrained
- **Statutory Plans**: Outline Zoning Plans (OZP), Development Permission Area Plans
- **Town Planning Board**: Quasi-judicial, public participation, appeals
- **Key Tensions**: Housing supply vs country parks, heritage vs redevelopment, height vs view corridors
- **Strategic Documents**: Hong Kong 2030+, Lantau Tomorrow Vision, Northern Metropolis
- **Innovation**: "Rail + Property" (MTR), underground spaces (caverns, logistics), brownfield rezoning

### Singapore: State-Led, Master-Planned, Long-Horizon
- **Statutory Plan**: Master Plan (5-yr review), Concept Plan (50-yr)
- **Agencies**: URA (planning), HDB (housing), LTA (transport), NParks (greenery), PUB (water)
- **Key Principles**: Liveable, sustainable, resilient, distinctive, inclusive
- **Strategic Documents**: Long-Term Plan Review (2022), Green Plan 2030, Climate Adaptation
- **Innovation**: Underground Master Plan, District Cooling, Pneumatic Waste, Smart Nation sensors

## The "Climate Decision" Factor

### Choose Hong Kong If You:
- Want **seasons** — cool winters, distinct autumn/spring
- Love **hiking/mountains** — real elevation, wilderness 30 min from office
- Tolerate **typhoons** — dramatic, disruptive, part of rhythm
- Accept **worse air quality** — regional, improving but still
- Value **harbor/waterfront** — Victoria Harbour iconic, working harbor

### Choose Singapore If You:
- Want **consistency** — no wardrobe change, predictable weather
- Prioritize **clean air** — consistently better, health-critical
- Fear **typhoons/extreme wind** — outside belt, only rain/haze
- Value **curated nature** — parks, connectors, reservoirs, accessible
- Accept **perpetual heat/humidity** — 24/7/365 tropical, AC life

## Future Geography

### Hong Kong: Northern Metropolis + Lantau Tomorrow
- **Northern Metropolis**: 300km², 2.5M population, 650k jobs — I&T, logistics, housing (San Tin/Lok Ma Chau, Kwu Tung North)
- **Lantau Tomorrow**: 1,700ha reclamation, 260-400k flats, 200k-340k population — 2030s+, strategic reserves, controversy
- **Brownfield**: 1,200ha agricultural/industrial — rezoning for housing (Kwu Tung, Hung Shui Kiu)
- **Underground**: Cavern Master Plan — 48 sites, logistics, data centers, reservoirs

### Singapore: Long Island + Greater Southern Waterfront + Underground
- **Long Island**: East Coast reclamation (800ha) — coastal protection + reservoirs + new town (2040s+)
- **Greater Southern Waterfront**: 1,000ha (post-2030 port move) — 100k+ homes, new CBD extension, waterfront living
- **Jurong Lake District**: 360ha — 2nd CBD, 100k homes, 150k jobs, HSR/RTS hub
- **Underground**: Rock caverns (ammo, oil, water), future: logistics, data centers, utilities
- **Cross-Border**: RTS Link (Woodlands-JB 2026), HSR (deferred, future)

## Bottom Line

**Hong Kong**: Dramatic topography, seasonal climate, typhoon theater, wilderness at doorstep, density forced by geography. For those who want seasons, hiking, harbor views, and can manage summer intensity.

**Singapore**: Managed tropical consistency, curated green, no typhoons, rising seas the existential challenge, planned spaciousness. For those who want predictability, clean air, outdoor accessibility, and accept perpetual summer.

**Climate Change Winner**: Singapore (better resourced, planned, coastal protection funded). **Hong Kong** has steeper adaptation curve (density, slope, water dependency) but stronger natural buffers (country parks).
